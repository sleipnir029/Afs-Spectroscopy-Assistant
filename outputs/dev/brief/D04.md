# Short brief: Glucose and lactate trends in an aqueous bioprocess

*Short view built by code from the saved run JSON (no model call). Full answer: [D04.md](../D04.md).*

**Provisional feasibility assessment:** methods worth testing under the stated conditions, not a final configuration.

Test a Raman fiber probe first: it suits aqueous media and has published precedent for direct glucose and lactate detection in reactors. Test NIR transmission/transflection alongside it as a comparison, since NIR is the most common online fiber method but needs a matrix-specific calibration. Combine the two only if a single method misses your accuracy targets. Open points: required accuracy and response time, turbidity range, fluorophore content and probe installation constraints.

## Measurement concept

Immersion Raman fiber probe and, in parallel lab tests, an NIR transmission/transflection probe with controlled pathlength; a combined or co-located probe pair only if fusion is pursued. **Location:** In situ in the reactor or a representative bypass loop; exact port to be fixed with the customer. **Fiber link:** Silica fibers carry the Raman excitation/collection and NIR ranges; the usable range depends on the spectrometer, fiber and probe actually selected, not on the nominal method range. [S01 p.15]

## Spectral range

- **Raman:** Raman shift in cm-1 with laser excitation wavelength stated separately; the usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe actually selected. [S01 p.11]
- **NIR:** Nominal NIR range 780-2500 nm; the usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe actually selected. [S01 p.10]

## Risks and uncertainty

- Fluorescence background from medium components can swamp the weak Raman signal and must be checked on real samples. [S01 p.11]
- The medium and cell population change during the run, so a calibration built on one phase may not hold across the whole process. [REF §5]
- Changing turbidity and scattering can mimic composition changes, especially in reflection-type measurements. [S01 p.7]

## Feasibility plan

- **Next step:** Run the multi-method lab trial on representative samples from your process with matched laboratory reference measurements, starting with the Raman probe.
- **Reference measurements:** Existing laboratory glucose and lactate measurements, with their precision quantified.
- **Decision basis:** Customer-defined accuracy, detection limit and response-time criteria evaluated on held-out runs or batches.
