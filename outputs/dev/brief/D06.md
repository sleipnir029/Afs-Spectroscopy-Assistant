# Short brief: Water content in a petroleum stream

*Short view built by code from the saved run JSON (no model call). Full answer: [D06.md](../D06.md).*

**Decision status:** Provisional feasibility assessment

Test mid-IR ATR first: it gives selective water bands and laboratory spectra of water in petroleum are shown in the supplied material. Compare it with NIR transmission, which suits long fiber runs to an analyzer outside the process area. Open points: the water concentration range and required precision, the analyzer-to-process distance (mid-IR fiber length is undocumented), stream opacity and temperature, and the reference lab method. A first lab comparison on representative samples with independent water references should establish which method meets your targets.

## Measurement concept

Immersion ATR fiber probe in the petroleum stream (primary); transmission/transflection probe with controlled pathlength as the NIR comparison. **Location:** Probe at or near the process line; spectrometer in the analyzer room outside the process area, connected by fiber. **Fiber link:** NIR: silica fibers enable remote industrial process control. MIR: CIR/PIR fibers required, but no source states an achievable link length, so the distance must be confirmed with AFS. [S02 p.6]

## Spectral range

- **MIR:** Nominal mid-IR range 4000-400 cm-1; the usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe actually selected. [S01 p.9]

## Risks and uncertainty

- No source states an achievable length for a mid-IR fiber link, so the analyzer-outside-the-area requirement may not be met with MIR-ATR. [S02 p.10]
- ATR probes sample only a surface layer a few micrometres deep, so fouling or a non-representative surface would bias the water result. [S01 p.13]
- If the stream is too opaque or fat-rich, NIR transmission loses the signal and the comparison option fails. [S01 p.18]

## Feasibility plan

- **Next step:** Run a multi-method laboratory trial on representative petroleum samples with water as the reference target, comparing MIR-ATR and NIR transmission before selecting equipment.
- **Reference measurements:** Laboratory water determination on every sample (method per an open clarification question).
- **Decision basis:** Go/no-go on customer-defined accuracy and detection limit for water, validated on held-out samples.
