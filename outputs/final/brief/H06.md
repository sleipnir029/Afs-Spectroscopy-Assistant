## Measurement concept

Fiber-optic NIR diffuse-reflection probe in light contact with the tissue surface (as in the supplied study, with a fixed probe-to-sample distance); later optionally a combined MIR-ATR/fluorescence probe. **Location:** First: excised tissue samples in the laboratory. In-vivo surgical use is a later, separately regulated step. **Fiber link:** Vis-NIR silica fibers cover the NIR range; the usable range depends on the spectrometer, fiber and probe actually selected. [S03 p.4; S02 p.6]

## Spectral range

- **NIR:** Nominal NIR range is 780-2500 nm; usable range depends on the instrument, fiber, and probe selected. [S01 p.10]

## Risks and uncertainty

- Ex vivo frozen/thawed tissue spectra can differ from real in vivo measurements, so ex vivo results may not transfer to surgery. [S03 p.3]
- The published model rests on very few patients and exploratory validation, so its statistics may not reproduce on new tissue. [S03 p.10]
- LED wavelengths optimized for kidney tissue may carry no discriminative information for a different organ or tumor type. (engineering judgement, no AFS source)

## Feasibility plan

- **Next step:** Obtain histopathologically confirmed ex vivo tissue specimens and perform benchtop NIR diffuse-reflection spectroscopy across the expected biological variability.
- **Reference measurements:** Histopathological classification of each sample (tumor vs healthy, grade) as independent reference.
- **Decision basis:** Customer-defined sensitivity/specificity thresholds on held-out samples; no go/no-go numbers exist in the sources.
