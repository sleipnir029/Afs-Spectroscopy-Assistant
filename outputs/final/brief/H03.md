# Short brief: A fluorescence instrument requested for glucose and viable biomass

*Short view built by code from the saved run JSON (no model call). Full answer: [H03.md](../H03.md).*

**Decision status:** Provisional assessment (the customer's premise is not supported by the sources)

Fluorescence alone cannot report glucose: glucose is not fluorescent and you have no marker or reference relationship, so that premise does not hold. Test Raman first on representative broth samples against lab glucose and viable-count references; NIR transmission is the comparison if the broth is transparent enough. Your fluorescence spectrometer may still serve a biomass-related signal via intrinsic fluorophores such as NADH or riboflavin, but that must be confirmed first. Required accuracy and go/no-go criteria remain open.

## Measurement concept

Immersion Raman fiber probe for the fermenter broth (primary test); fluorescence only via a fiber probe if a fluorophore is confirmed; NIR transmission/transflection as comparison if the broth is transparent enough. **Location:** In the fermenter or a bypass sampling point, same spot for any combined measurement. **Fiber link:** Silica fibers suit Raman and NIR; the existing fluorescence spectrometer's fiber compatibility is unknown. No link length can be stated from the sources. [S01 p.11]

## Spectral range

- **Raman:** Raman shift in cm-1 with laser excitation wavelength stated separately; the usable shift range depends on the instrument, laser and probe actually selected. [S01 p.11]
- **NIR:** Nominal NIR range 780-2500 nm; the usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe actually selected. [S01 p.10]

## Risks and uncertainty

- The broth's fluorescence background could swamp the weak Raman signal, degrading the primary glucose measurement. [S01 p.11]
- Any glucose or biomass model is matrix-specific and must be re-validated if the medium, organism or process changes. [REF §5]
- Rising turbidity during fermentation can saturate transmission geometries and force a switch to reflection or ATR. [S01 p.16]
