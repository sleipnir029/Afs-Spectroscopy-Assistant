# Short brief: Reuse of a silica link for petroleum MIR ATR

*Short view built by code from the saved run JSON (no model call). Full answer: [H04.md](../H04.md).*

**Decision status:** Provisional assessment (the customer's premise is not supported by the sources)

The concept cannot be approved as stated: silica fibers cannot carry mid-IR light, so MIR ATR needs a CIR or PIR fiber instead of the existing cable. MIR ATR itself is worth testing first, with a published precedent for water in petroleum. Open items: the MIR link length to the other building, the sample state, and the required accuracy.

## Measurement concept

Immersible MIR ATR fiber probe (ZrO2 or diamond ATR tip) in contact with the petroleum stream, coupled via CIR or PIR fiber to an FTIR spectrometer. **Location:** In-line on the petroleum line; analyzer location depends on the achievable MIR fiber link length, which is unknown. **Fiber link:** CIR or PIR fiber; the existing silica cable cannot be used for MIR. Achievable MIR link length is an open question. [S02 p.13; S01 p.10]

## Spectral range

- **MIR:** Nominal MIR range 4000-400 cm-1; usable range depends on the selected CIR or PIR fiber and ATR crystal, not to be confused with an instrument or fiber range. [S01 p.9]

## Risks and uncertainty

- The MIR fiber link to another building may not be achievable at the required distance, as no source states an achievable industrial MIR link length. [S02 p.10]
- ATR samples only a surface layer a few micrometres deep, so crystal fouling and surface representativeness in a flowing petroleum line are risks. [S01 p.13]
- The physical state and optical regime of the petroleum are unknown, so the ATR contact requirement and Table 1 sample fit are unconfirmed. [S04 p.4]

## Feasibility plan

- **Next step:** Supply the link length, routing and operating conditions, and run the laboratory MIR ATR feasibility test on representative petroleum samples with Karl Fischer reference values.
- **Reference measurements:** Karl Fischer titration or an equivalent laboratory water-in-oil method, supplied by the customer.
- **Decision basis:** Go/no-go criteria (required accuracy, detection limit, response time) to be defined numerically by the customer; the plan proceeds only if the MIR link length is confirmed feasible.
