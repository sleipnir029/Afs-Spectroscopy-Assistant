- **Slide text (verbatim)**: text printed on the slide, copied as written, typos included. Only this part should be quoted as S02 wording.
- **Figure (our description)**: what plots and photos show, in our own words. It is not a quote.
- **Uncertain readings**: values that are hard to read, or that disagree with other sources.
