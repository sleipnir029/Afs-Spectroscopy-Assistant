Round 2: hybrids of A (Technical report) and C (Engineering dossier)

Three ways to combine A and C. Each fixes the problems found in the round-1 UX audit (ux-audit.md). All variants use the app’s colour tokens and its self-hosted IBM Plex Sans and Mono, and add IBM Plex Serif for display text only. Content is copied from outputs/dev/D03.json (option O2). The drawings are hand-written SVG for the mockup. In the app they stay code-drawn, with a wide and a narrow version, as diagrams.py does today.

What is shown: the desktop slice at 1440 px, then for each variant its fonts, a dark-mode swatch and the real 390 px phone column (the same markup, collapsed by container queries). P2 is shown in its hover state on the drawing and in the table. In H1 the first citation chip shows a keyboard focus ring, and in H3 it shows the quote preview.

Your question

Our dairy process includes a relatively clear whey stream before a concentration stage. We want to investigate whether spectroscopy can track protein concentration as the stream changes, with laboratory results used as the reference. The stream can become more turbid under some operating conditions. We need a first measurement concept that handles this change rather than assuming the liquid always remains clear.

1Our recommendation

Start with NIR transmission/transflection.

The sources name whey as a preferred case for it, and it is the least effort. Because the stream can turn turbid, keep mid-IR ATR as the fallback and treat a fused NIR plus mid-IR ATR model as a later hypothesis. The protein range, required accuracy and the nature of the turbidity are still open.

Mediumopen questions Q1, Q2
  • M1
    NIR, transmission/transflection probe · primary. Applies while the stream stays clear enough for transmission with a controlled optical pathlength.
  • M2
    MIR, ATR probe · conditional. Applies only if the turbid operating state defeats NIR transmission.

Why this option: covers clear and turbid operating states. Cost drivers: NIR and MIR spectrometers, MIR fibers, ATR probe.

ParameterNeedMeasured byWhySource

5Open questions

  1. Q1
    What protein concentration range must be covered, and what accuracy and response time are required?Why it matters: These decide whether one method can cover the target and they set the go/no-go criteria for the test.
  2. Q2
    How turbid does the stream become, and what causes the turbidity (particles, fat or dissolved species)?Why it matters: The severity and nature decide whether NIR transmission still works or whether ATR is needed.
  3. Q3
    Where is the measurement point, is it in-line in the pipe, in a bypass or at-line, and how far is the analyzer from it?Why it matters: This fixes the probe type and the fiber link, which differ between NIR and mid-IR.
