Turns after new facts are marked illustrative, because no real re-run exists yet, and they only reuse claims from the real run.

We want to investigate whether spectroscopy can track protein concentration as the stream changes, with laboratory results used as the reference. The stream can become more turbid under some operating conditions. We need a first measurement concept that handles this change rather than assuming the liquid always remains clear.

You

Assistant

NIR reflection is the usual dairy route for opaque or concentrated products3, but this whey stream is normally clear, and reflection is less representative for clear liquids4.
