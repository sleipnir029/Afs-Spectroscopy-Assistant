You · enquiry

Our dairy process includes a relatively clear whey stream before a concentration stage. We want to investigate whether spectroscopy can track protein concentration as the stream changes, with laboratory results used as the reference. The stream can become more turbid under some operating conditions. We need a first measurement concept that handles this change rather than assuming the liquid always remains clear.

Assistant · v1 · 13 min 42 s

Start with NIR transmission/transflection.

The sources name whey as a preferred case for it1, and it is the least effort. Because the stream can turn turbid, keep mid-IR ATR as the fallback2 and treat a fused NIR plus mid-IR ATR model as a later hypothesis.

You

Why not NIR reflection? That’s what we usually sell for dairy.

Assistant

NIR reflection is the usual dairy route for opaque or concentrated products3, but this whey stream is normally clear, and reflection is less representative for clear liquids4. Screening also ruled it out: S01 Table 1 lists it for powder and scattering media. The turbid state is covered by the mid-IR ATR fallback instead.
